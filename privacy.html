Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This policy explains what data nezzbot ("we", "us") stores, why, how long we keep it, and how you can get it deleted. It covers the bot and the web dashboard. By using them, you agree to this policy.

1. What we store

We store only what the features need. Most of it is Discord IDs, settings and text that server admins and members put in themselves.

Server settings

  • The IDs of servers, channels, roles and users that your settings refer to.
  • The settings of each feature: prefix, limits, whitelists, log channels, autoroles, welcome texts, custom commands and their aliases, reaction roles, verification, moderation reply texts, staff roles, mute roles and fake permissions.
  • The bio that the server owner sets for the bot on their server. The avatar and banner they upload go to Discord and are not stored by us.

Moderation

  • Cases: warns, notes, mutes, bans, kicks and jails, and when they were lifted. Each has the member's ID, the moderator's ID, the reason or the text of the note, and the time. Notes are only visible to moderators and the member is not told about them.
  • Jail: the ID of a jailed member and the roles that were taken away, so they can be given back when the member is released.
  • Anti-nuke events: who did what, when, and the punishment that was applied.
  • Server backups, when anti-nuke is on: a copy of the structure of the server (channels, categories, roles, permission overwrites, names and order). They contain no messages and no member lists.

Message logs

  • If a server turns on message logging, we store the content, the author's ID, the channel ID and the attachment links of deleted and edited messages for 7 days.
  • For the snipe commands, the last few deleted or edited messages of a channel are kept for up to 2 hours. Only members with the Manage Messages permission can see them.
  • Other messages are only read in memory, for example to catch spam or blocked links, and are not stored.

Features that members use

  • Levels: user ID, experience, level and the number of messages counted. Not the messages themselves.
  • Tickets: the number, channel, who opened it, its status and times, who claimed or closed it, the reasons given, who was added, the answers in the ticket form and the rating you give at the end.
  • Ticket transcripts: when a ticket is closed or deleted, up to its last 1000 messages are saved (time, author's name and ID, text, text of embeds and links of attachments) so the staff can read them in the dashboard even after the channel is gone. A copy can also be posted to a log channel.
  • Each transcript has a secret link. Anyone who has the link can read the transcript until the link expires, 30 days after the transcript was last saved.
  • Suggestions: the text, the author's ID (even if the suggestion is shown anonymously), the status, the reason, the reviewer's ID, and the IDs and votes of members who voted.
  • Giveaways: the prize, the host, who entered and who won.
  • Starboard: the IDs of starred messages, their authors and the copies. The copies are posted in Discord and stay there.
  • Counting: the current and best number and the ID of the last member who counted.
  • Reminders: the text, the channel and message you asked in, your ID and the time it is due.
  • AFK status: the reason you set and the time.
  • Last.fm: if you link your account, your Discord ID and your Last.fm username. We send the username to Last.fm to show what you listen to.
  • Voice rooms: the ID of a temporary room and its owner.
  • Booster roles: the booster's ID and the ID of the role made for them.
  • Sticky messages, automatic reactions and auto publish: the channels, texts and emojis that admins set, and the ID of the member who created a sticky message.
  • Custom commands and autoresponders: the triggers, replies, restrictions and actions that admins set, and the ID of the member who created them.
  • Premium: the server ID, the tier, the expiry and the ID of the Discord subscription. Payments are handled by Discord and we never see payment details.

Direct messages we send you

When a moderator warns, mutes, jails, kicks or bans you, the bot sends you a message with the action, the reason, the duration and the moderator. Depending on the server's settings, it can also message you when a ticket you opened is closed or reopened, to ask for a rating, when a reminder is due and when a suggestion you made gets an answer. We send these once and don't store them, apart from the moderation case itself.

The dashboard

  • When you log in with Discord, we receive your user ID, username, avatar and the list of your servers with your permissions there. We use it to show the servers you can manage.
  • Your session is stored on our servers and linked to a cookie. It ends when you log out, after 24 hours without use, or 7 days after you logged in.
  • Cookies: one for your session, one for the login process (10 minutes) and one that remembers your theme (1 year). We don't use advertising or analytics cookies.
  • Dashboard access: the server owner can pick people who may use parts of the dashboard. We store their user IDs and the access level of each part, until the owner removes them or removes the bot.
  • Configuration exports: the file is made when the owner asks for it and is sent to the owner only. We don't keep a copy and it contains no member data.
  • Drafts: while you edit a page, the unsaved values are shown to other managers of the same server who have the page open, and are kept for up to 5 minutes.
  • Permission checks: when you open a server in the dashboard, the bot checks your current permissions there. The result is kept for at most a minute, so losing a permission takes effect quickly.
  • Abuse protection: short counters per IP address, user and server (for example the number of requests, open tabs, or bans per minute). They expire within 10 minutes. Requests from the dashboard to the bot carry a random one-time identifier, kept for a minute, to stop replays. It holds no personal data.
  • Errors: when a dashboard page breaks, the error message, the page address and technical details are sent to us.

Running the service

  • Blocked users and servers: if we block someone for abuse, we keep the ID, the reason and the date.
  • We can message a user directly to answer a report. These messages are not stored.
  • To look into abuse or to give support, we can create a one-time invite, valid for one hour, to a server the bot is in. It only lets us join as a regular member.
  • Technical logs contain errors, Discord IDs and times. We don't put message content in them. Serious errors are sent to a private Discord channel of ours, and, if we have error tracking turned on, also to Sentry.
  • When the server owner changes the bot's profile, we log the server ID, the owner's ID, a hash of the new image and the new bio, so misuse can be investigated. The images are not kept.
  • Scheduled work such as ending a temporary ban, sending a reminder or ending a giveaway is tracked in a job queue. A job holds only its name, a server ID and a time.
  • We keep usage numbers, such as how many commands ran and how fast we responded. They contain no user or message content.

We don't collect email addresses, passwords, payment details or other people's direct messages. We don't sell data and we don't share it with advertisers.

2. Why we use it

  • To run the features that a server turned on.
  • To carry out the moderation and anti-nuke settings of a server.
  • To let admins change settings in the dashboard, and the owner change how the bot looks on their server.
  • To prevent abuse and fix errors.
  • To apply the benefits of premium.

3. Where it is stored and who can see it

Data is stored in a PostgreSQL database and a Redis cache that we run. Only we have access to them. Admins and staff of a server can see the cases, logs, tickets and transcripts of that server, depending on their permissions there. We use reasonable technical measures to protect the data, but no system is completely secure.

4. Who we share it with

  • Discord, because the bot works through its API.
  • Last.fm, if you link your account.
  • Discord's subscription system, for premium.
  • Sentry, if we have error tracking turned on. Error reports can contain IDs and technical details. We don't add message content to them.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

  • Message logs: 7 days. Snipe data: 2 hours. AFK status: 30 days, or until you write a message.
  • Anti-nuke events: 90 days.
  • Moderation cases: until the bot is removed from the server.
  • Levels, giveaway entries and other community data: until the server resets it or removes the bot.
  • Tickets, answers, ratings and transcripts: until the bot is removed, also after the ticket channel is deleted. The public link of a transcript stops working 30 days after it was last saved, but staff can still read the transcript in the dashboard.
  • Reminders: until delivered or removed. Suggestions and votes: until the server deletes them or removes the bot. Jail data: until the member is released.
  • Bot bio: until the owner removes it or removes the bot. Your Last.fm link: until you remove it.
  • Dashboard access lists: until the owner removes the people or removes the bot.
  • Sessions: until you log out, 24 hours without use, or 7 days after login. Counters and permission checks: from a minute up to 10 minutes.
  • Finished jobs are deleted after a day, failed ones after three days.
  • When nezzbot is removed from a server, we delete all data of that server right away. If the bot was offline at that moment, we delete it the next time the bot starts.

6. Your rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Contact us through the support server. We complete verified deletion requests within 30 days. You can also unlink Last.fm yourself, log out of the dashboard at any time, and a server owner can remove the bot to delete all data of their server.

7. Children

Discord requires users to be of a minimum age. nezzbot is not meant for children and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone below that age.

8. Changes

We may change this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. If you keep using nezzbot after a change, you accept the new policy.
